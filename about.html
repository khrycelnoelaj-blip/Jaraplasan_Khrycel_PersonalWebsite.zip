Hello, it's KC!

My full name is Khrycel Noela E. Jaraplasan, but you can call me KC. I am 18 years old and I live in Bay, Laguna.

I studied at Liceo de Bay during my junior high school years. Those years were some of my prime years because I was active in different school and community activities.

I continued my senior high school years at Trace College. I really enjoyed my high school life because I met a lot of new friends and new people. It's where I grew up and became more mature.

I love being surrounded by people who care about me, but I also value my alone time. I enjoy having quiet moments to recharge, reflect, and spend time with myself.
